# EZ-CDM 1.3.0

A flash can keep going while its line is true, a fill's color can shift with the amount, and the fill moments take "and".

## New
- Looks: "Flash it again and again": the flash repeats every 1.2 seconds while its line is true
- Looks: Flare around me offers Daylight UI 2.4.1's Strike animation
- Looks: A fill's color can shift with the amount, as Daylight UI's health bar does: green with much left, red with little, whichever way it fills. It works in a fight
- Lines: "While it's cooling down" and "While it's active" take "and" parts, and "it's on cooldown" and "its buff is up" are parts after "and" on other lines

## Changed
- Drawer: A flash line says "once" or "again and again"
- Drawer: A fill's color word offers "shifts with the amount" beside the accent and a picked color
- Looks: Fills show as a thin bar under the icon. Tint fills you made become thin bars
- Drawer: "While it's active" is offered only where Daylight UI can tell the buff is up

## Fixed
- "While it's active" now lights on a spell shown as "Cooldown and buff", such as Water Shield and Lightning Shield
