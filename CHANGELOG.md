# EZ-CDM 1.2.0

A spell's icon can fill up or drain with your power or its time left.

## New
- Looks: "Fill it with my power or time left": a tint rising over the icon or a thin bar under it, that drains or rises, in the accent or a color you pick. It works in a fight
- Lines: "Always", "While it's cooling down" and "While it's active" take the fill
- Time left follows the spell's cooldown, your own buff or your totem, and never counts the global cooldown

## Changed
- Drawer: Time left isn't offered on procs, buffs that never wear off or debuffs, and the drawer says why. A spell with nothing to fill with offers no fill
