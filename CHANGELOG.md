# EZ-CDM

## 0.1.0

First release. Needs Daylight UI 2.2.4 or newer, on WoW Forever.

- An EZ drawer in Daylight UI's Cooldown Manager editor (/cdm). Click a spell in your
  layout and the drawer writes its settings as plain sentences you change by clicking words.
- A start block beside the editor: EZ-Start Blank makes a new empty layout, and EZ-Load
  Layout loads one of your class's layouts, on a copy by default.
- "While it's active, glow" works in combat on buffs Daylight UI shows in its aura icons.
- Glow and play a sound a set number of seconds before a buff or debuff falls off.
- A glow style for each moment of a spell.
